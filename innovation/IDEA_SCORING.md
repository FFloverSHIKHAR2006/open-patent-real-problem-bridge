# Idea Scoring Framework

Score each idea from 1–10.

## Criteria

- Problem Severity
- Demand
- Innovation
- Technical Feasibility
- Evidence
- Cost
- Scalability
- Impact

## Formula

Overall Score =
Problem Severity × 0.20
+ Demand × 0.15
+ Innovation × 0.15
+ Feasibility × 0.15
+ Evidence × 0.15
+ Cost × 0.05
+ Scalability × 0.05
+ Impact × 0.10

## Priority

Prioritize ideas with high problem severity, evidence, feasibility, and impact while maintaining meaningful novelty.
