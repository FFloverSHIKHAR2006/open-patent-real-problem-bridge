# Research Hub

## Research Objective

Discover how existing public innovation can be connected to real-world operational problems.

## Research Questions

1. What problems are currently underserved?
2. What technical solutions already exist?
3. Which solutions are hidden in patents/research?
4. Which mechanisms transfer across industries?
5. Where are the largest innovation gaps?
6. Which problems have strong demand?
7. Which solutions are technically feasible?
8. What prevents existing solutions from being adopted?

## Research Loop

Discover → Analyze → Identify Gap → Generate Hypothesis → Validate → Refine → Repeat
